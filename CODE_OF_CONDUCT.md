# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Cued's community to be welcoming, constructive, and safe for everyone.
Participation in this project means treating other contributors with respect,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or the media services they use.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions before escalating a
  disagreement.
- Respect privacy: never share credentials, private media details, personal
  data, or screenshots that identify people without permission.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, repeated bad-
faith disruption, or publication of another person's private information are
not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
Reports will be handled as discreetly as possible. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and participation that conflict with this code.
